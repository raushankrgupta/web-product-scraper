Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-09-15

1. Introduction

Welcome to TryOnFusion ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access our mobile application (the "App") and our website (the "Site").

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, references, is capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with you ("Personal Information").

A. Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Gender, Age, Height, Weight (for fitting purposes)

B. Media & Photos (Sensitive Data)

Our App collects user photos to enable the "Virtual Try-On" feature.

  • What we do: We upload your full-body photo, the clothing and background images you choose, and any photos of other people you add for a couple or group try-on, to our secure servers to generate the try-on image.
  • Storage: These images are stored securely on AWS S3.
  • Deletion: You can delete your photos at any time via the specific deletion option in the Gallery or by deleting your account.

C. Sign-in Information

If you sign in with Google or with Apple, we receive your name and email address from that provider, and a provider account identifier. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address from Apple instead of your real email address. For Sign in with Apple we also keep a token issued by Apple, used only to revoke our access when you delete your account.

D. Purchases

Stars are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store processes the payment; we never receive your card, UPI, or bank details. We receive and keep the store's transaction or purchase identifier, the product bought, and its status (for example completed or refunded), so we can add stars to your account and handle refunds.

3. Third-Party AI Processing

To generate a try-on image, the photos described in section 2.B, the body details saved in the person profile being used (gender, height, weight and measurements), and any styling note you type are sent to an AI image-generation provider that processes them on our behalf and returns the result. Each image is generated by one of:

  • Google (Gemini API)
  • OpenAI (OpenAI API)

These providers receive only what is needed to create the image you asked for — not your email address or account details. They process it under their API terms for business customers. The App asks for your permission before any photo is sent for AI processing, and you can decline; you will then not be able to generate try-ons. You can withdraw permission by contacting us, and delete your photos at any time as described above.

Only upload photos of other people who have agreed to it.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the functionality of the App (specifically, Virtual Try-On).
  • Manage your account, your star balance, and provide customer support.
  • Communicate with you about updates or offers (if you opted in).

We do not sell your personal information, show you third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

5. Link Import (in-app browser)

When you open a link with Link Import, the page is loaded on your device in a private window that does not share cookies or logins with your normal browser and is cleared when you close it. We do not receive your browsing activity, the page content, cookies, or anything you type on that website.

When you tap Import, we receive only the images you selected, the page address, and the page title, which are stored in your account so you can use them for try-ons. For service quality we record the website's domain name and the number of images imported.

6. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data.

  • In-App: Go to Profile -> Settings -> Delete Account.
  • Web: Please contact us at support@tryonfusion.com to request deletion if you cannot access the app.

Upon deletion, all your personal data and uploaded images are permanently removed from our active databases, and if you used Sign in with Apple we revoke our access with Apple. We keep a one-way hash of your email address (not the address itself) to prevent the sign-up bonus being claimed repeatedly, and purchase records where the law requires us to keep them.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may contact us at:
